Bot Photo Animator

Put animated bots on a photo and export a short video. Everything runs in your browser; the photo never leaves your computer. Bots are rendered with a port of bloub, measured from x.ai's bot video. Unofficial.

  1. Choose a photo
  2. Add bots, drag them into place
  3. Give each one animations, export

    Selected bot

    No bot selected. Click one on the canvas or in the list above.

    The badge is the blue dot that pops up in the “notify” animation.

    Face — the expression the bot wears whenever it is idle. Hover a face to see it move.

    Where it looks — normally each animation decides. Turn “hold gaze” up to make the bot look in the direction you set instead, for example at the person in the photo.

    Animation timeline — plays top to bottom; the last one holds until the video ends. Idle blocks can carry their own face.

    Add an animation — click to append it. Hover to see it play.

    Export

    Video export needs Chrome (it uses the browser's own H.264 encoder) and loads a small MP4 muxer from a CDN the first time. Your work autosaves in this browser; “Save project” writes a file that includes the photo.
